Frequently Asked Questions

Have Questions? We're Here to Help!

Find answers to our most commonly asked questions. Can't find what you're looking for? Contact our customer service team.

Orders & Payment

How can I place an order?

You can place an order through our website by browsing our collections, adding items to your cart, and proceeding to checkout. We accept various payment methods including credit cards, debit cards, and digital wallets.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, UPI, and Paytm. All transactions are secured with industry-standard encryption.

How can I track my order?

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number via email. You can also track your order through our Order Tracking page.

Shipping & Delivery

Do you offer free shipping?

Yes! We offer free shipping on all orders above ₹10,000 within India. For orders below ₹10,000, a flat shipping rate of ₹250 applies.

How long will my delivery take?

Delivery times vary by location: 3-5 days for metro cities, 5-7 days for other cities, and 7-10 days for remote areas. See our Shipping Information page for details.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we ship to select countries. International shipping costs and delivery times vary by location. Please contact our customer service for specific details.

Returns & Exchanges

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy for unworn items in original condition with all tags and certificates. Visit our Returns & Exchanges page for full details.

How do I return an item?

Contact our customer service to initiate a return. We'll provide a return authorization number and shipping instructions.

When will I receive my refund?

Refunds are processed within 7-10 business days after we receive your return. The amount will be credited to your original payment method.

Product & Care

Are your products certified?

Yes, all our Jewellery comes with authentication certificates and hallmarks as per Indian Jewellery standards.

How should I care for my Jewellery?

Store pieces separately, avoid contact with chemicals, and clean with a soft cloth. For detailed care instructions, refer to the product care guide included with your purchase.

Do you offer repairs?

Yes, we offer repair services for our Jewellery. Contact our customer service team to arrange a repair assessment.

Account & Security

How do I create an account?

Click the profile icon in the top right corner and select 'Sign Up'. Fill in your details to create your account.

Is my personal information secure?

Yes, we use industry-standard encryption to protect your personal and payment information. We never share your data with third parties.
