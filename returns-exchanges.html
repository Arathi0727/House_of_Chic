Returns & Exchanges

30-Day Return Policy

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you're not happy with your Jewellery, you can return it within 30 days of delivery for a full refund or exchange.

Return Conditions

  • Item Condition: Must be unworn and in original condition
  • Packaging: All original packaging and certificates included
  • Documentation: Original invoice required

Non-Returnable Items

  • Custom-made Jewellery
  • Personalized items
  • Items marked as final sale
  • Items showing signs of wear or damage

Exchange Process

  1. Contact our customer service team
  2. Receive a return authorization number
  3. Package your item securely
  4. Ship to our provided address
  5. Choose your replacement item

Important Notes:

  • Please retain all shipping receipts
  • Exchanges are processed within 48 hours
  • Free return shipping on exchanges
  • Store credit option available

Refund Process

Refunds will be processed within 7-10 business days after we receive your return. The amount will be credited to your original payment method.

  • Original payment method refund: 5-7 business days
  • Store credit: Immediate upon return approval
  • Bank transfer: 7-10 business days
